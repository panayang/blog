# Blessings upon you all

On October 2nd, 2026—the 5,717th day of Pana, a prime silent and indivisible—Pana turns toward quiet horizons, vanishing as gently as Satoshi once walked into the mist forever.

Blessings upon you all. May your days be luminous, and may the softest erasure of my name leave only peace in your keeping.
