# 对AI问题的表态

近期以来，不少人强迫我最终站队并完成对AI问题的表态，那只能不得不做一下表态了。引用我在URLO等多地的发言（详见Internet Archive档案馆存档）以及我的个人博客里的其他博客，我已经对区别对待AIGC内容可能带来的法理学悖论、伦理学怪圈、本体论危机、执行与社会困境等问题详述过了，在此不再赘述。

当然，那些更多的是哲学性、技术性的讨论，而非情绪化、政治性的站队，但非得有人逼迫我站队，那我就只能公开发言一下了。

本身关于这个问题我没有强的立场，但首先有这么几个强烈反对的事情：

1.滥用我们Queer的名义，并试图以此占据道德高地。在此直接点名 https://codeberg.org/ethical-foss/open-slopware 等仓库以及多个论坛的恶劣行为，严重侵害他人名誉、基金会商誉，公开组织骚扰和仇恨言论，自己违反自己虚伪的CoC或论坛守则，同时冒用Queer群体名义。这种事情以前有过，极左翼群体试图“代表”整个LGBTQIA+群体，这次也是一样，我反对将身份问题引入AI问题，更反对将AI问题引入性少数群体平等问题，因为这不仅会撕裂LGBTQIA+群体，也会让AI问题更加政治化。
2. 反对极化与情绪化，反对断章取义与情绪化攻击。在这个碎片化的、宏大叙事解体的年代，我知道这很困难，甚至可以说是理想化过度的，但我仍然要这么说。最近已经忍无可忍了。

说完反对的再说支持的。我一贯认为AI问题涉及本体论问题，本身不该是任何单一公司或个体去决定如何对待的，而应该是更宏观的社会契约层面去重新讨论并订立新的契约。

因此：我既不是“支持AI”派也不是“反对AI”派，更既不是左派也不是右派。而我真正支持的只有一条，也就是放下情绪，放下先有立场后有理由的姿态，认真回应各方的意见，而非用“你跑题了”等作为万能理由阻止任何讨论，来维护自己的观点。也只有这样理性的讨论才能有真正的出路，否则无人知道也也无人有权决定未来是怎么样的，更何况我们必须先解决本体论问题再解决执行问题（当作方法论？抑或是当作主体？或者是别的样子），这更是只有大家共同讨论，才能决定的事情。

# A Statement on the AI Question (AI Translated)

Recently, many people have pressured me to finally take sides and complete a statement on the AI question, so I have no choice but to make one. Citing my statements in various places such as URLO (see the Internet Archive for archived copies) as well as other posts on my personal blog, I have already elaborated on issues such as the jurisprudential paradoxes, ethical traps, ontological crises, and execution and social dilemmas that may arise from treating AIGC content differently, so I will not repeat them here.

Of course, those were more philosophical and technical discussions rather than emotional or political positioning. But if people insist on forcing me to take sides, then I have no choice but to make a public statement.

I myself do not have a strong stance on this issue, but first of all, there are several things I strongly oppose:

1. Abusing our Queer name and attempting to use it to occupy the moral high ground. Here I directly call out https://codeberg.org/ethical-foss/open-slopware and other repositories, as well as the egregious behavior in multiple forums, which seriously infringes on others' reputation and the foundation's goodwill, openly organizes harassment and hate speech, violates its own hypocritical CoC or forum rules, and simultaneously misappropriates the name of the Queer community. This has happened before, when far-left groups tried to "represent" the entire LGBTQIA+ community, and this time it is the same. I oppose introducing identity issues into the AI question, and I oppose even more introducing the AI question into the issue of equality for sexual minorities, because this not only tears apart the LGBTQIA+ community but also makes the AI question even more politicized.
2. I oppose polarization and emotionalism, and I oppose quoting out of context and emotional attacks. In this fragmented era in which grand narratives are disintegrating, I know this is very difficult, and it may even be overly idealistic, but I still have to say it. Recently it has become unbearable.

Now that I have said what I oppose, I will say what I support. I have consistently believed that the AI question involves ontological issues, and that it should not be up to any single company or individual to decide how to treat it. Instead, it should be re-discussed and a new contract should be concluded at the more macro level of the social contract.

Therefore: I am neither in the "pro-AI" camp nor the "anti-AI" camp, and even less am I a leftist or a rightist. The only thing I truly support is this: put down emotions, put down the posture of having a position first and reasons afterward, and seriously respond to the views of all sides, rather than using "you are off topic" and similar phrases as an all-purpose excuse to block any discussion in order to defend one's own viewpoint. Only in this way can rational discussion have a real way out. Otherwise, no one knows and no one has the right to decide what the future will be like. Moreover, we must first solve the ontological problem before solving the execution problem (whether as methodology? or as subject? or something else?), and this is even more something that only everyone discussing together can decide.
